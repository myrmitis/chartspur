Estimates are not this product

The free chart table publishes a rank heuristic (dl_day=20000*(50/rank)^0.7). That curve is not calibrated to store data. On the paid chart it can overstate downloads by a large factor. Those dollar figures are not included in this brief and are not something you are paying for. Do not use them for a forecast, a valuation, or a store payout.
